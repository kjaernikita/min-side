INTERESTS

i am curious about how technology, people and good processes come together. both professionally and personally, i am drawn to things where there is something to understand, improve or create.

[01] devops

i find devops really interesting, especially the process of setting up environments and making sure everything works together properly. i like the combination of systems, structure and problem solving, and it is something i have become more interested in through my work.

[02] quality engineering

through my work, i have developed a strong interest in testing, quality and reliability. i like the idea that systems should not only work, but work consistently and correctly when they are needed.

[03] product & problem solving

i am very interested in how ideas turn into products that create real value. when working on a project, i try to focus on the actual problem, the people affected by it and whether the solution is genuinely useful, not just technically interesting.

[04] reformer pilates

reformer pilates is one of my favourite ways to reset outside of work and university. i like having something physical that gives me a break from studying and sitting in front of a computer.

[05] cooking & baking

i love cooking and baking, especially things that take a bit of patience. i also really enjoy preserving food, pickling and making things that can be stored and used later.

[06] time with my boyfriend

i really value spending time with my boyfriend and doing small everyday things together. we like going to the cinema, taking walks, cooking together and having cosy evenings at home. we also collect pokemon cards, which has become one of the hobbies we enjoy sharing.
